C++17 · solver-neutral fatigue module

Tensor normalization and stress history

The pipeline preserves the full 3D tensor until a uniaxial method explicitly requests a scalar projection.

Canonical symmetric tensor

\[\mathbf{T}=\begin{bmatrix}T_{xx}&T_{xy}&T_{xz}\\T_{xy}&T_{yy}&T_{yz}\\T_{xz}&T_{yz}&T_{zz}\end{bmatrix}.\]

Recognized labels include SXX, E11, S12 and EYZ. The normalizer maps labels independent of importer order and rejects ambiguity, duplicates and missing components.

Engineering and tensor shear

\[\gamma_{xy}=2\varepsilon_{xy},\qquad \varepsilon_{xy}=\frac{\gamma_{xy}}{2}.\]

Engineering shear is halved before assembling the tensor. Unknown strain shear convention is a hard error, not guessed from the field name.

Coordinate transforms

Let \(\mathbf R\) contain local unit axes as columns expressed globally:

\[\mathbf v_g=\mathbf R\mathbf v_l,\qquad \mathbf T_g=\mathbf R\mathbf T_l\mathbf R^{\mathsf T}.\]

The inverse uses \(\mathbf R^{\mathsf T}\). The implementation checks finite values, orthonormality and \(\det\mathbf R=+1\). A missing/invalid transform is an explicit failure.

Invariants and principal values

\[\sigma_m=\frac{\operatorname{tr}(\boldsymbol\sigma)}{3},\qquad \mathbf s=\boldsymbol\sigma-\sigma_m\mathbf I.\]
\[\sigma_{\mathrm{VM}}=\sqrt{\frac12[(\sigma_{xx}-\sigma_{yy})^2+(\sigma_{yy}-\sigma_{zz})^2+(\sigma_{zz}-\sigma_{xx})^2]+3(\tau_{xy}^2+\tau_{xz}^2+\tau_{yz}^2)}.\]

Principal values are the descending eigenvalues of the symmetric tensor:

\[\lambda_1\geq\lambda_2\geq\lambda_3,\qquad \det(\mathbf T-\lambda\mathbf I)=0.\]

These invariants do not replace the tensor history. Principal directions can rotate and component phase relationships matter under multiaxial loading.

History construction

\[\mathcal H(p)=\{(t_k,\mathbf T_k,\mathcal S_k)\}_{k=0}^{m-1},\qquad t_{k+1}>t_k.\]

One history refers to one fixed FEM position \(p\), one quantity (stress or strain), one unit and one target frame. Each entry retains time, source step index, normalized tensor and raw source sample. No interpolation, resampling, time sorting or unit conversion is performed.

Explicit uniaxial projection

\[q(t)=\mathbf n^{\mathsf T}\mathbf T(t)\mathbf n,\qquad \|\mathbf n\|_2=1.\]

The direction is finite, normalized and expressed in the history frame. It is supplied by the caller; no critical-plane search occurs.

Scalar Rainflow

The implementation collapses consecutive plateaus while retaining the last time/index, extracts local reversals and processes them with a three-point stack. Closed ranges are emitted with weight 1.0; residual segments are half cycles with weight 0.5. For each pair of endpoints:

\[\Delta q=|q_b-q_a|,\qquad q_m=\frac{q_a+q_b}{2},\qquad q_a^{\mathrm{amp}}=\frac{\Delta q}{2}.\]

Cycle endpoints retain their original sample indexes and physical times.

This scalar counter does not filter noise, apply hysteresis thresholds, synthetically close endpoints, resample irregular histories or count multiaxial tensor paths. It does not perform critical-plane search.
